About Cyber Shield

A Machine Learning Approach to Safeguard Healthcare Systems in Software-Defined Networking (SDN) Environments.

Project Overview & Objectives

The healthcare sector processes highly sensitive patient records, medical device telemetry, and life-critical diagnostics. As healthcare networks adopt Software-Defined Networking (SDN) to achieve centralized management and dynamic routing, the control plane and OpenFlow switches become prime targets for sophisticated cyber threats.

Cyber Shield leverages ensemble machine learning models trained on network telemetry to classify traffic in real time into 9 distinct categories, detecting intrusions before network disruption or data exfiltration occurs.

🔐

Brute Force & Auth Attacks

Detects automated credential-stuffing and password-guessing attempts against healthcare admin portals.

⚡

DDoS (TCP / UDP)

Identifies packet floods designed to saturate OpenFlow controller buffers and disrupt medical device connectivity.

💉

SQL Injection & Web Exploits

Captures exploit payloads attempting to extract electronic health records (EHR) through backend database queries.

🔍

Probe & Port Scanning

Uncovers reconnaissance operations seeking open ports and vulnerable healthcare services across the SDN fabric.

📂

Samba & Protocol Exploits

Flags unauthorized network storage and SMB/CIFS protocol manipulations across clinical workstations.

🖥️

VNC & Remote Hijacking

Detects rogue remote desktop tunnels and unauthorized screen control sessions in hospital environments.
